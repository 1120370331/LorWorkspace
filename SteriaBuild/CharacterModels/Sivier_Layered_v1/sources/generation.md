```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Production asset for a modular Library of Ruina-style 2D character. ONE landscape 1536x1024 RGB PNG with THREE separated invisible vertical columns. Pure flat chroma magenta #FF00FF background, no gradients, checkerboard, ground shadows, scenery, labels or text. All parts completely inside their own columns with a generous gutter. Preserve the reference's compact approximately 3-head-tall miniature proportions, cel-painted broad color shapes, refined charcoal outlines. Render all three figures at the SAME anatomical scale. Do not use 3D/plastic rendering.
BODY-AND-EQUIPMENT ONLY. No head, hair, eyes, face or head decorations. End at a short neck stump just above the collar, not a long neck. Draw the body, limbs, fingers, garments and correctly held physical weapons as connected coherent POSES. Keep fixed garment trim, belts and buckles painted naturally into each pose. No magic, motion trails, floating shield, water ribbons or particles baked into this sheet.
ALL action directed toward viewer's LEFT in a slight THREE-QUARTER FRONT view, consistent across every pose. No pure profile pose. Distinct weight shifts, bent elbows, knees and planted soles; no rotating a straight standing outfit to fake movement. Both feet and all weapon tips must fit within the cell.
Character: Sivier, a gentle capable female fencing defender. Keep the reference's black/navy fitted school-like jacket with muted blue piping, white high-collar blouse and small dark bow, navy pleated skirt with blue hem, slim silver belt and tiny gold details integrated into the clothes, short white socks and black low shoes. One elegant slim silver RAPiER with a small gold swept guard and round pale-blue gem, no broad sword, no second weapon. Keep the same rapier proportions in all poses. Her free hand is expressive for shielding allies. Modest practical outfit exactly like reference, no redesign.
Left DEFAULT: relaxed narrow planted stance, rapier held safely downward toward LEFT with blade tip just above ground, free arm relaxed by side. Middle GUARD: defensive fencing guard toward LEFT, wide grounded stance, knees softly bent, sword hand holds rapier diagonally up-left across front of chest and free palm braces near ribs, shoulder and elbow anatomically connected. Right SPECIAL: shield-gathering ally-support gesture, planted stable feet, rapier raised diagonally up-left with bent sword elbow while the free palm opens forward-left at chest height to offer protection; composed, open supportive silhouette. No actual shield or effects.
```
